# Costs

**Costs** are what the farm pays, sorted by what makes them grow. A chick
costs the same whether the farm raises three hundred birds or three
thousand, and the insurance costs the same whether it raises any.

The sorting decides what contribution means. Subtracting the costs that grow
with the flock from revenue leaves what each bird earns toward the bills
arriving regardless. Put one of those bills in the wrong class and the
contribution figure still divides cleanly into a draw goal, and still
produces the wrong number of birds.

## Cost classes

| Class | What moves it |
|---|---|
| Per bird | The flock. These belong in the cost of the goods sold. |
| Per market day | How often the stall goes out. |
| Per dollar sold | Sales, whatever produced them. |
| Per year | Nothing. These are paid whether or not a bird is raised. |

Two of the three lines are invoiced. Feed is the exception, and the reason

Sausage processing costs $16.19 a
bird<sup class="dec d-verified" data-tip="Verified" tabindex="0">+3</sup>.
Sausage processing is charged on the birds diverted to sausage rather than
on all of them, so it is held out of the per-bird total above.

### Feed

The price is invoiced. A 2,500 lb pallet costs $932.58 for broiler mash and
$869.45 for layer pellets, with $119.00 of shipping on either, which puts
the rate at $0.4206 and $0.3954 a
pound<sup class="dec d-verified" data-tip="Verified" tabindex="0">+3</sup>.

Two readings of feed per bird disagree. The first is a season's spend
divided by the birds it fed. The second is the published standard for the
breed.

| Reading | Feed per bird | Cost per bird |
|---|---:|---:|
| What the farm fed<sup class="dec d-projected" data-tip="Projected" tabindex="0">P</sup> | 10.8 lb | $4.54 |
| What the breed asks<sup class="dec d-projected" data-tip="Projected" tabindex="0">P</sup> | 15 lb | $6.31 |
| **Gap** | **4.2 lb** | **$1.77** |

Closing that gap takes every bird from $18.12 to $19.89. Whether it also
raises the weight the bird reaches, and so what it sells for, is a question
for the [goods measure](/measures/goods).

The feed invoices are deliberately not read into this repository. The ruling
was made on August 26, 2026, and it accepts a known gap instead of carrying
a document that would need scrubbing. What is missing is the monthly total.
The rate above is invoiced.

## Per year

These arrive on a calendar instead of on a bird.

| Cost | A year |
|---|---:|
| Farm liability insurance<sup class="dec d-verified" data-tip="Verified" tabindex="0">+3</sup> | $330 |
| Fuel, vehicle and equipment<sup class="dec d-assumed-high" data-tip="Assumed, high confidence" tabindex="0">+2</sup> | $1,660 |
| Bedding<sup class="dec d-assumed-low" data-tip="Assumed, low confidence" tabindex="0">−2</sup> | $200 |
| Digital and administrative<sup class="dec d-unknown" data-tip="Unknown" tabindex="0">−3</sup> | Not costed |
| Electricity<sup class="dec d-unknown" data-tip="Unknown" tabindex="0">−3</sup> | Not costed |
| Grit and oyster shell<sup class="dec d-unknown" data-tip="Unknown" tabindex="0">−3</sup> | Not costed |
| **Costed so far** | **$2,190** |

Three of those six lines carry no figure, so the total is a floor and not a
sum. They are listed and left blank, because a list with an invented number
in it reads as finished and a list with a hole in it does not.

The insurance receipt is confirmed and the coverage is not. The policy
predates the broiler enterprise and does not cover it, so a verified figure
sits against inadequate cover.

The vehicle line covers markets, pop-ups and the round trips to processing.
Equipment is diesel and gasoline through the season.

Bedding covers the brooder across a season. The winter storage house runs a
deep layer whose volume nobody has measured.

The digital line covers the workspace subscription, hosting, the domain and
print. None of it has ever been totalled.

## Selling costs

Two costs grow with selling instead of with raising, so neither belongs in
the per-bird total or the per-year one.

Market vendor fees are real and unquantified. They vary from market to
market, and no reading exists to size
them.<sup class="dec d-unknown" data-tip="Unknown" tabindex="0">−3</sup>

Payment processing takes a percentage of each sale plus a fee on every
transaction. It is filed here instead of with the digital line, because
grouping it there would understate the cost at volume and overstate it
below.<sup class="dec d-unknown" data-tip="Unknown" tabindex="0">−3</sup>

## Held out

A part-time helper at 11 hours a week costs $11,440 a
year<sup class="dec d-assumed-high" data-tip="Assumed, high confidence" tabindex="0">+2</sup>.
The helper is held out of the total because a wage is a choice rather than a
bill.
